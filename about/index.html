The story

A one man army

I've been creating for as long as I can remember, and these days every frame that goes out with my name on it goes through me.

Cutting, adding music, making the final cut: I do all of it. I focus on the details and the tiny stuff people usually miss, because that's what turns raw footage into a video people actually finish.

Editing turned into a business. That business turned into tools. Now I'm building CreativeFlow, so other creators can plan, script and grow without the chaos I had to figure out alone.
